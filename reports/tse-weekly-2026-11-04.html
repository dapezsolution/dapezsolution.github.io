Tehran Stock Exchange: a five-week low, then a two-day rebound

Week of 31 October to 4 November 2026 (9 to 13 Aban 1405) · Published 5 November 2026 · DapezSolution research, Tehran

Key points

  • The main index fell to about 7,654,000 on Sunday, its lowest level since 30 September, then rebounded on Tuesday and Wednesday.
  • Over the week the index lost about 0.7% (approx.), ending near 7.91 million. Closing levels for 2 to 4 November were not reported; the week-end level is from the weekly summary used in our note of 11 November.
  • Individual investors took a net 8,300 billion tomans out of equities. Tuesday brought the first clear inflow in two weeks.
  • The free-market dollar held around 272,000 tomans and eased by 4,000 to 5,000 tomans on Wednesday.

2. What drove the week

Macro uncertainty kept selling pressure high on Saturday and Sunday, with about 70% of symbols lower on Saturday and the heaviest individual selling in basic metals and oil products on Sunday. Monday was mixed: the main index closed slightly lower, but the equal-weight index rose. Demand returned on Tuesday, when the index gained about 175,000 to 177,000 points, and the rebound continued on Wednesday as the dollar eased and petrochemicals rose 1.25%.
