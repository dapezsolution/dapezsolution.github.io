Tehran Stock Exchange: the main index slips below 8 million as individuals sell

Week of 24 to 28 October 2026 (2 to 6 Aban 1405) · Published 29 October 2026 · DapezSolution research, Tehran

Key points

  • The main index fell about 2.5% (approx.) to around 7,967,000, its first weekly close below 8 million in this log. Several daily levels are rounded or calculated in the sources.
  • Individual investors took a net 16,914 billion tomans out of equities on the four days with figures; Wednesday's flow was not reported.
  • The free-market dollar rose to about 269,810 tomans on Wednesday, about 2% above the previous week, after reaching 272,465 on Tuesday.
  • Higher perceived macro risk drove the selling, especially on Saturday and Tuesday.

2. What drove the week

Saturday's fall came with a doubling of the individual outflow as perceived macro risk rose. Sunday was calmer, with the equal-weight index positive and attention on the reopening of the refining group. On Tuesday individual selling picked up again as the dollar rose and global gold retreated ahead of a US monetary policy decision; petrochemicals were one of the few positive groups. On Wednesday the market recovered an early fall of about 75,000 points to close higher.
