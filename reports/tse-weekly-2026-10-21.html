Tehran Stock Exchange: the main index falls about 3% while smaller shares gain

Week of 17 to 21 October 2026 (25 to 29 Mehr 1405) · Published 22 October 2026 · DapezSolution research, Tehran

Key points

  • The main index (TEDPIX) fell about 251,800 points, or about 3.0% (approx.), to 8,173,767 over the five trading days. Closing levels for 17 to 20 October are rounded in the sources.
  • The equal-weight index rose about 1.3% (approx.) to about 2,255,000. The losses were concentrated in the largest companies.
  • Individual investors took a net 11,561 billion tomans out of equities. Only Monday and Tuesday saw net inflows.
  • On Wednesday the free-market dollar opened at 264,610 tomans and 18-carat gold was 26,796,000 tomans per gram.

2. What drove the week

The week opened with heavy selling on Saturday, when 706 symbols fell and only 163 rose. Breadth then improved quickly: on Monday 702 symbols rose and 152 fell, and the equal-weight index gained on four of the five days. The main index, which is dominated by the largest companies, ended the week lower because selling in those heavyweights continued, including on Wednesday.
