Tehran Stock Exchange: the main index gains 6.5% on record individual inflows

Week of 7 to 11 November 2026 (16 to 20 Aban 1405) · Published 12 November 2026 · DapezSolution research, Tehran

Key points

  • The main index (TEDPIX) rose about 515,000 points, or 6.51%, from around 7,912,000 to 8,427,305 over the five trading days. [1][2]
  • The equal-weight index rose about 5.7%, close to the main index. Gains were spread across the market, not limited to the largest companies. [1]
  • Individual investors added a net 19.6 trillion tomans (approx. US$75 million) to equities over the week. Tuesday alone brought 11.9 trillion tomans, which local press called a record. Sunday was the only day with a net outflow. [1]
  • The free-market US dollar eased to 259,910 tomans on Wednesday, from around 272,000 a week earlier. Measured in dollars, the main index gained roughly 11% (approx.). [3]
  • On Wednesday the index touched an intraday record near 8.54 million, then gave back about two-thirds of the day's gain. Trading value was high and individuals sold more per person than they bought, a sign of profit-taking. [1][2]

3. What drove the week

The week was driven by money coming in from individual investors, and by a weaker dollar. Most of the gain came on Tuesday, when lower macro tension pushed the free-market dollar down and buyers moved into equities across the board. [1]

The main index and the equal-weight index rose by similar amounts. That points to broad demand rather than a rally carried by a few large companies. [1]

Inflation is the background to every nominal gain. The Statistical Centre put annual inflation for October at 76.8%, while the Central Bank's figure is 71.4%. [5] This is why the dollar-terms change, roughly +11% this week, is a cleaner measure of real performance than the index in tomans.

4. Where the money went

On Wednesday, the largest individual inflows went to three industries: [2][4]

  • Chemicals and petrochemicals: more than 1 trillion tomans
  • Oil products: about 317 billion tomans
  • Basic metals: about 207 billion tomans

These are export-oriented industries whose revenues are linked to foreign currency. On Wednesday, 481 symbols rose and 291 fell.

5. Trading value and market size

Wednesday's trading value in shares, rights and equity funds was 58.1 trillion tomans (approx. US$224 million). [4] That is a high level of turnover, and it came on a day when individual buyers were less active than sellers. Local press estimated the market's total value at about US$94 billion at Wednesday's close.

6. What to watch

  • Whether individual inflows continue, after Wednesday's shift to more selling per person than buying.
  • The area around 8.5 million points, where Wednesday's intraday record met profit-taking.
  • The free-market dollar. This week's gains came alongside a weaker dollar, and a reversal would change that picture.
  • November inflation figures from the Statistical Centre.
