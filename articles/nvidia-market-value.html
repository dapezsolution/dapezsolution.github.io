How big is Nvidia? A $4.2 trillion company in context

Source published 15 November 2026 · Brief by DapezSolution research, Tehran

The figures

Donya-e-Eqtesad published an infographic putting the market value of Nvidia, the US designer of processing chips, at 4.2 trillion dollars, among the largest companies in the world. It compares that with other large listed companies:

CompanyMarket value, as shownNvidia as a multiple
Nvidia$4.20 trillion 
Meta$1.64 trillionabout 2.6×
Tesla$1.50 trillion2.8×
JPMorgan$0.91 trillionapprox. 4.6×
Reliance Industries (India)18.07 trillion rupees 

Multiples for Meta and Tesla are as stated in the source. The JPMorgan multiple is our own calculation. The graphic says Reliance is worth about 0.05 times Nvidia; it is shown in rupees and is not converted here. No date is given for the data.

The outlet attributes the company's growth to demand for advanced chips and the infrastructure behind artificial intelligence.

Why it matters

Our view. For comparison, local press put the whole Tehran Stock Exchange at about US$91.5 billion this week, roughly one forty-sixth of this single company. The scale of a few technology companies in world markets is one reason swings in AI shares now move global risk appetite, the dollar and commodity prices, which in turn reach Iran's export industries.
