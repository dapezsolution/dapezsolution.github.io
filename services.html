Made to order

Any report, built around your question

If what you need is not listed, ask. We write custom reports on any topic in Iran's economy and markets, at the depth and frequency you choose.

  • One-off report
  • Daily, weekly or monthly updates
  • English or Italian
  • Web report, Word or Excel

Request a custom report

For example

  • An industry or product category
  • A commodity, such as steel, petrochemicals or agricultural goods
  • The exchange rate, inflation or the budget
  • A new rule or regulation and what it changes
  • A province or city as a market
  • A data series you cannot find in English

Custom reports follow the same rules as all our research: sourced figures, neutral language, and no buy or sell recommendations on listed securities.

How a project works

  1. You send the question and any background by email.
  2. We agree scope, timeline and a fixed price.
  3. We research, using primary Persian sources and, where needed, interviews.
  4. You receive the report and a follow-up call to discuss it.

Payment terms are agreed in each contract. Every client and project is screened against applicable sanctions lists before work begins, and legal, registration and accounting work is carried out by licensed local professionals. Services are not available to U.S. persons. We do not provide investment advice or assist with any activity that breaches applicable sanctions.
