Methodology

Where our numbers come from

Trust in research comes from knowing how it was made. This is how we work.

Sources

  • Official market data from the Tehran Stock Exchange and its data services.
  • Statements and statistics from government bodies, such as the Food and Drug Administration, the Central Bank and the Statistical Centre.
  • Iran's established financial press, and international outlets for global context.
  • Company filings, used to build industry-level totals. Individual companies are not named.

Rules we follow

  • Every figure is linked to a source and a date.
  • When sources disagree, we show both figures.
  • Our own calculations, such as US dollar equivalents, are labelled "approx." with the exchange rate used.
  • If a number is not available, we say so rather than estimate it silently.
  • Iranian calendar dates are converted to the Gregorian calendar.

What we do not do

  • Recommend individual securities or give personal investment advice.
  • Take political positions.
  • Help anyone avoid or breach sanctions.
