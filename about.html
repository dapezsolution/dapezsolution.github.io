About

Dara Pezeshkian

Founder of DapezSolution. Investor and trader on the Tehran Stock Exchange for more than 15 years.

I have traded Tehran-listed equities and options for more than fifteen years, through several currency crises, policy changes and market cycles. Before that I worked in export brokerage, and I have lived and worked in the United States.

That combination is the reason DapezSolution exists. I know how Iran's market and economy work day to day, and I know which parts are hard to see from outside: the different exchange rates, administered prices, the trading rules and the gap between official and real figures.

I also founded TehranBridge, which helps European companies, particularly from Italy, understand the Iranian market before they enter it.

How I work

  • Primary sources first, mostly in Persian, checked against each other.
  • Every figure is sourced. Estimates are labelled.
  • Neutral on politics. The analysis is about economics and markets.
  • Industry-level research only. No tips on individual stocks.
