How to read these numbers

  • TEDPIX is the main, value-weighted index; the equal-weight index gives every company the same weight and shows the breadth of the market.
  • Net individual flow is the money individual investors put into or took out of equities; it is the most watched sentiment gauge in Tehran.
  • With annual inflation of about 70 to 77%, gains in tomans overstate real returns. Compare the index with the free-market dollar.
