Iran economy this week

Updated 14 Nov 2026
  • 11 NovBudget: oil still accounted for roughly one-third of planned government funding in the 2026-27 budget proposal. A Plan and Budget Organization deputy said the government must prepare for oil revenue reaching zero.Plan and Budget Organization, via Al Jazeera
  • 10 NovInflation: 76.8% annual and 88.7% year on year in October, says the Statistical Centre. The Central Bank puts annual inflation at 71.4%. Food prices are up 113% on a year earlier.Statistical Centre of Iran / Central Bank, via Euronews
  • 8 NovPetrol: the monthly quota is 160 litres in three price tiers, the last 50 litres at 10,000 tomans per litre. The full quota now costs 740 thousand tomans, up from 490 thousand, about 51% more.National Iranian Oil Products Distribution Company, via Euronews
  • 8 NovFood vouchers: about one million households are removed in the first stage. The income ceiling is 27 million tomans for a four-person household.Ministry of Cooperatives, Labour and Social Welfare, via Euronews
  • 8 NovMedicine sales reached about 400 trillion tomans in the first half of the Iranian year. Local producers supplied 98.9% of units, imports 13% of value.Food and Drug Administration, via Mehr
